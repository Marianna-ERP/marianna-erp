# MARIANNA ERP - Update 2026-05-28

This build applies the requested corrections after the first Shipments / Logistics module.

## Purchase Orders -> Inventory validation

Purchase Orders are now blocked from transferring information into Inventory when any PO line has:

- missing product
- quantity empty or zero
- unit price empty or zero

When a PO is saved in a non-draft status, the system validates all lines before creating Expected lots in Inventory. If validation fails, the user receives a detailed alert and no Inventory lot is created.

## Sales Orders -> Inventory cancellation reversal

Sales Orders are no longer physically deleted from the workflow. Cancelling an SO now:

- changes the SO status to `Cancelled`
- releases reservations automatically because Cancelled SOs are no longer included in reservation calculations
- reverses Inventory only when the cancelled SO had already generated a `SHIP_OUT` movement
- adds an Inventory `REVERSAL` movement so the audit trail remains visible

This avoids silently losing commercial records and keeps Inventory balanced.

## Sales Order email workflow

Sales Orders now have an email workflow similar to Purchase Orders:

- print/PDF document preview
- email draft modal
- editable recipient, subject and message
- `mailto:` email draft opening from the browser

Draft SOs are still blocked from being emailed.

## Transport Order confirmation

The Transport Order document is now bilingual English / Polish and includes:

- MARIANNA company logo
- company details
- carrier / forwarder details
- loading and unloading locations
- customs location
- temperature requirements
- goods and freight
- truck / driver / container / BL / AWB table
- bilingual operational terms
- signature blocks for MARIANNA and carrier

The structure follows the uploaded road transport order facsimile, including the Polish legal / operational clauses and cold-chain requirements.

## Transport Order email workflow

Shipments now have direct email preparation for transport orders:

- print/PDF transport order
- email order modal
- editable recipient, subject and body
- `mailto:` email draft
- status can be marked as generated/sent

This is intentionally the same browser-based workflow used by the PO/SO modules. A real SMTP/API sending flow can be added later.

## Shipment statuses and modes

Header shipment statuses are now reduced to:

- Booked
- Confirmed
- Loaded
- Arrived
- Delivered
- Closed
- Cancelled

Header shipment modes are now:

- Road
- Sea
- Rail
- Air
- Multimodal

Leg modes now include:

- Road
- Sea
- Rail
- Air
- Warehouse

## Multi-truck / multi-container tracking

Shipments now support multiple transport units per leg.

Each shipment can have many legs, and each leg can have many transport units. A transport unit can represent:

- one truck
- one trailer
- one container
- one air waybill / air unit
- one rail unit

Each unit tracks its own:

- quantity kg
- pallets
- truck plate
- trailer plate
- driver name
- driver phone
- container number
- seal number
- booking number
- BL number
- AWB number
- vessel / voyage
- notes

This solves cases such as 4 sea containers arriving at port and then being split into 5 road trucks due to European road weight limits.

A new seed scenario was added:

`SHP-2026-0070` - 4 potato containers arriving by sea and then split into 5 trucks from Gdansk port to WH-01 Poznan.
